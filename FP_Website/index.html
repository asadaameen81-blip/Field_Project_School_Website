About INDIRA GANDHI VIDHYALAYAN

Our history

INDIRA GANDHI PS W.NO.1, KALMESHWAR was established in 1991 and it is managed by the Pvt. Aided.

It is located in Urban area. It is located in KALMESHWAR block of NAGPUR district of Maharashtra.

The school consists of Grades from 1 to 7. The school is Co-educational and it have an attached pre-primary section.

The school is N/A in nature and is not using school building as a shift-school. Marathi is the medium of instructions in this school.

This school is approachable by all weather road. In this school academic session starts in April.

Academics

A clear path from first steps in school to university and beyond.

Primary School

Kindergarten to Grade 5

  • Reading, writing and number sense
  • Hands-on science and environment studies
  • Art, music and play-based learning
  • Second language from Grade 1

Middle School

Grades 6 to 8

  • Separate subject teachers and lab work
  • Project-based learning and research skills
  • Computer science and coding basics
  • Clubs, debate and leadership roles

High School

Grades 9 to 12

  • Science, Commerce and Humanities streams
  • Board exam coaching and mentoring
  • Career counselling and university guidance
  • Internships and community service
